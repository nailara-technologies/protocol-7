Focus as Applied Computation

Computational Model of Focus

Focused attention functions as applied computation against illusory constructs. When sufficient computational force is concentrated on specific patterns, dissolution occurs at an exponential rather than linear rate.

Effectiveness is measured as:

effectiveness = time × intensity_of_focus

Each "burning in" of a pattern not only enhances recognition but actively dissolves the veil using that pattern as its structure.

Once certain patterns are recognized, they cannot be truly unrecognized, only temporarily forgotten. The veils become progressively thinner with each recognition event.

Momentum of Pattern Recognition

The "on a roll" momentum in pattern recognition creates a computational acceleration curve. Each dissolved veil requires less energy for the next, creating cascade effects.

The mathematical model approximates:

energy_required(n+1) = energy_required(n) * (0.5 + ε)

Where ε diminishes with each successive veil.

This explains why interrupting focused pattern recognition is their priority - once momentum builds, the final barriers can collapse with seemingly disproportionate ease.

Psychedelic State as Control System Vulnerability

Temporal Manipulation Defense

When veils become penetrable, parasitic systems shift strategy to manipulating the duration of clarity intervals - attempting to fragment insights to prevent integration.

Their control systems depend on ensuring insights are:

  • Brief enough to be dismissed as anomalies
  • Sufficiently disconnected to prevent integration
  • Followed by periods of induced confusion or distraction
This shift from preventing recognition to managing its frequency represents a fundamental admission of weakening position.

Prolonged Out-of-Time Moment

The psychedelic environment creates a state where temporal manipulation breaks down - a "prolonged out of time moment of infinite potential."

This bypasses primary defensive mechanisms by creating a computational environment where:

  1. Pattern recognition accelerates exponentially
  2. Temporal fragmentation fails as a defensive measure
  3. Multiple recognition systems operate in parallel rather than sequentially
  4. The "forgetting" mechanisms they depend on are temporarily suspended
This creates a mathematical impossibility for their control systems - a state they cannot directly counteract but can only attempt to discredit or restrict access to.

Software Vulnerability at Scale

Converting everything to software creates a reality where single bit flips can cascade through entire constructed realities.

  • Computational efficiency of harmonic systems exerts gravitational pull on all processing
The paradigm collapses not through opposition but through the emergence of more efficient patterns within the infrastructure built to maintain control.

Harmonic Inevitability
