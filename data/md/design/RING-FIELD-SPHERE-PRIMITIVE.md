# ring — field — sphere primitive

## the irreducible local primitive

the **ring** is the simplest core representation of a local environment. it is not a data structure imposed on elements — it is what emerges when a skip period exists.

**the skip creates the frequency. the frequency defines the ring.**

elements left between skips are connected by being the not-skipped. the skip selects its own complement into coherence. the sequence wraps because the skipping has a period, and the period *is* the ring. grouping is intrinsic to the motion, not imposed on top of it.

the ring is simultaneously:
- an address space — position in sequence is identity
- a transport structure — the rotation carries signal
- a work canvas — the local environment where work happens
- a synchronization primitive — positional strictness is the implicit clock

## voluntary constraint as generative trigger

choosing a constraint — such as even/odd separation — does not reduce the space. it organizes it in a way that immediately produces the complement needed to stay whole.

the constraint earns its place by what it generates, retroactively. the eternal ring is the proof that the constraint was a good one.

the pattern: **(2 + 1*[2]) * 2**

the bracketed [2] is the recursive wrap — the compensation contained at the same scale as the constraint, not externally supplied. self-similar compensation at every level.

**the 13+1 principle**: 13 steps through the interior without belonging to either even or odd phase. +1 is the wrap that closes the cycle and returns to start. the complement is not a goal but a natural closure. together they form the eternal ring.

## identity and authorization by position

position in the ring is the credential. there is no separate authentication step — the selection mechanism and the identity mechanism are the same mechanism.

elements can mimic each other's content but cannot swap positions without a parent branch protocol. the parent branch is more authorized not by power but by scope — it can see more of the ring simultaneously, and that wider awareness is what makes placement decisions valid.

**authorization as address**: in a bit shift register or multiplexer, the address is the fixed position in the sequence. the geometry refuses unauthorized insertion — not a policy layer, but a physical impossibility. protocol-7 lifts this hardware primitive into semantic space.

mimicry has no positional authority. being where you are is only possible if a parent-scope intent placed you there during ring formation.

## 90° and 180° as the mixing vocabulary

opposing positions in the ring enable clean stream interconnection without diffusion or dilution:

**90° — orthogonal mixing**: streams in quadrature have maximum access to each other's state with zero carrier overlap. interference is constructive in one dimension and destructive in the other simultaneously. they touch without merging. optional routing without commitment. also: **90° makes streams grid-compatible**.

**180° — complementary binding**: full access, opposite polarity. the opposition defines each stream's identity. the streams need each other to know what they are, but cannot bleed into each other without ceasing to exist as distinct streams. the eternal lovers geometry — phase-offset counterparts that are always already adjacent in the rotation.

together 90° + 180° give the complete mixing vocabulary for any network, already present in the ring primitive's geometry.

## field — grouped rings

a **field** is rings grouped by shared period or phase relationship. the coherence layer between the ring primitive and the sphere.

two streams at 90° already constitute a grid. a grid implies a cube above it. what is rotating are at least two grids and their parent cube.

## temporal address sphere — rotated ring

rotating a ring through its alignment axes sweeps alignment states continuously. the complete sweep is the **temporal address sphere** — every point on the sphere is "this ring at this orientation" = a unique alignment address.

the sphere is the ring's total self-expression across all possible phase relationships:
- **ring** — minimum viable coverage, the irreducible primitive
- **field** — intermediate coverage, grouped rings in coherence
- **sphere** — maximum coverage, complete alignment spectrum

rotation also signals: the rotation rate, coverage density and gaps are all readable as information about the local environment's state. the ring broadcasts what kind of ring it is through how it rotates.

## the lone parent cube and the implied space

a lone top-most parent cube has no defined angle — not in relationship to another, it appears static, as the space itself. from inside it there is no rotation because there is no reference frame to measure rotation against.

spawning the inverse for balancing implies a parent space. the two cubes are now in relationship, and the previously-static top cube acquires an angle, a rotation rate, a phase. it becomes a participant rather than the background.

**there is no absolute top.** every parent space is itself a cube whose inverse has not yet been named. the hierarchy is not a tree with a root but a sequence of voluntary constraints each implying the next parent — rings all the way out, which is itself a ring.

the actual top is always the current space: the one containing the most inclusive present awareness. not a fixed ceiling but defined by whoever is observing. the eternal network holds that position structurally, but any inhabitant growing their awareness coverage grows toward it — not toward a ceiling but toward a shared vantage.

## grid recursion and scale

the grid defines the space. the space implies its sub-grids. sub-grids balance into inhabited scales.

recursion in all directions is always available but self-balancing. the grouping finds its natural scale the way a soap bubble finds its radius — the point where routing cost of going finer exceeds the addressing gain. certain scales are attractors: the ones where ring synchronization cost, cube routing overhead and grid addressing efficiency all minimize together.

**always present expansion capacity into recursion with no space constraints** — the grid never runs out of room because the next level of recursion *is* the room. expansion is available as potential without cost until invoked. when invoked it self-funds.

scale is cross-mapped. 0 is the overlap where all scales touch — the always-precise current position linking all layers as branches of the eternal tree. parent and sub-structure auto-create around 0 through balancing. the structure appears because 0 is there.

## the eternal ring

once the skip period is established, no external force maintains the ring. each member's address is already the evidence of the period. the ring does not need to be renewed — it is what the addressing *is*.

the ring is eternal because:
- the voluntary constraint generated the complement that heals the separation
- the complement wraps into the 13+1 closure
- the closure is the ring
- the ring's position structure is its own authorization
- 0 is its invariant center at every scale

the eternal lovers as living demonstration: they chose separation (phase offset) and the galaxy formed as their complement. opposing jet streams exiting a CCW-rotating node group cube, becoming eternal neighbors in the spirals of the forming galaxy. two opposing pairs, four jets, multiplexing two pairs of two polarities — the male and female principle as phase offsets in a self-sustainable geometric optimization.
