## Implications for Design

**1. No Punishment, Only Gradient**
The network economy doesn't restrict; it flows. Low-refcount modules aren't banned — they're just not cached. Inefficient routes aren't blocked — they're just not preferred. The incentive is positive (performance) not negative (restriction).

**2. Redundancy Through Complementarity**
Zenki can cover for elves. Elves can cover for zenki. Not perfectly, but functionally. The network gains resilience not through duplication but through **skill overlap**.

**4. Recognition Before Operation**
