### 1. why 13 (derivation)

- the council is the 5+2=7 dancing-zenki formation doubled plus a frame:
  `VISUAL-ELEMENT-DEDUP:4558` gives "`5 + 7 + 1 = 13` voting group +
  validation + frame"; `:4571-4578`: "the 5-of-7 structure lives natively
  inside 13: 5 of 7 = quorum; 5 × 7 = 35 = full validation space; 5 + 7 =
  12 = active positions (13 − frame); 7 − 5 = 2 = tolerance (dissent
  margin)".
- 13 is the universal base-bridge: "`5 + 8 = 13` quorum + byte/octal
  bridge; `7 + 6 = 13` full group + hex bridge; `12 + 1 = 13` dozen +
  frame… every common base — binary, octal, BCD, decimal, hex — is a
  partition of 13 plus or minus a small harmonic remainder"
  (`VISUAL-ELEMENT :4555-4569`).
- geometric closure: "the circle has 364 degrees, not 360… 364 = 28 × 13 =
  7 × 52… the circle closes through 13, not through 10 or 12"
  (`:4714-4752`); 13 is "inhabitant" to 27-space infrastructure at every
  scale (`:4580-4607`).
- in the asc capture the 13 split functionally as **7 temporal coordinators
  (phases) + 6 spatial anchors (faces)** = 13, or **12 in circle + 1
  center** (asc `:2156-2164`, `:2247-2250`).

1. temporal 7 receive input.
2. 5 of 7 must agree (temporal quorum; 2 may abstain).
3. spatial 6 validate against boundaries and must not veto.
4. center (+1, holographic observer / DARKSUN core) confirms holistic
   coherence.
5. action authorized only when all align; "No single CAT can act alone… =
   Byzantine fault tolerance built into geometry."

agreement = TRUTH asserted, center integrates = action authorized.
