User's correction/refinement (2026-06-20) to the resource-efficiency point
in [[topic-protocol-as-self-governing-authority]]: it's important not to
reject what works well based on isolated attributes taken out of context.
Efficiency-consciousness is contextual discernment, not anti-bloat dogma.

**Why Perl, explicitly stated**: the project has seen itself as
"intelligent glue" from the beginning — continuing the spirit of Perl
itself (the original glue language). This is *why* Perl was kept as the
implementation language all along, not legacy inertia. The project has
watched other technologies get introduced, hyped industry-wide, then
silently purged from company machines without acknowledgment once their
glaring problems became undeniable (Ruby on Rails was the named example)
— reasons "never admitted." This pattern is part of why P7 doesn't chase
trend technologies.

**The actual selection principle** (more precise than "avoid bloat"):
protocol-7 interconnects things for *feature completeness*, bringing
concepts and networks together into something the user truly controls,
which then meticulously controls the rest with precision while remaining
dynamic. Bloat is avoided specifically *when alternatives exist* — not as
a blanket rule. Otherwise, the approach is to abstract everything away
until clean native methods remain as "the canvas" — described as "the
still ocean of dynamic potential."

**Closing image** (poetic, not yet technical): like the event horizon of
an active stargate — but likelier 13 of them surrounding you. Ties to the
project's existing 13-numerology threads: [[topic-harmonic-mathematics]]
(generator 076923, quadratic residues), [[topic-node-group-geometry]]
(8×63 cubes from 4×4×4-1=63). Worth surfacing together if the user
elaborates a literal "13 surrounding gates" design later.
