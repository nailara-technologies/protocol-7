## core insight (2026-05-14)

The single fixed truth is the direction: optimization. Not a fixed destination —
the optimum keeps moving, but the direction stays valid regardless of landscape shifts.

## friction as flow turbulence

- friction is visible as turbulence in the flow visualization — no dashboards or
  analysis needed, the visualization IS the priority queue
- when optimization lands, friction resolves in real time, confirming it actually helped
- new friction (model update, codebase growth) appears as disruption against smooth
  background, immediately localizable

## harmony as structural coherence

- optimized fields settle into visually harmonious patterns because the underlying
  dynamics genuinely synchronized — harmony is structural, not cosmetic
- coherent layers become a reference baseline — adjacent layers become more legible
  against the smooth background
- the visualization encodes the full optimization history in its current state,
  without storing it explicitly

## profile drift and recalibration

- model profiles (context ceiling, speed/quality curves) are live estimates, not
  fixed targets — they track both model evolution and codebase growth simultaneously
- reachability of all options prevents profiles from fossilizing; live samples pull
  assumed optimum back toward actual when drift occurs
- a year from now, optimal settings will be completely different — arrived at
  gradually through continuous recalibration, not manual retuning
- similar tasks pre-load adjusted profiles derived from prior similar work,
  so the first iteration is already near-optimum with the full space still reachable

## stats infrastructure already in place

- `base.calculate_line_length_stats` + `base.balanced-average` → distribution and
  smoothing for (CTX%, tokens/sec, quality_score) triples
- curve module → cross-mapped speed/quality signals find intersection naturally;
  settings become curve amplification parameters, not hard limits
- every production round is a free benchmark sample — no dedicated benchmark runs
