Avoid em-dashes (—, `&mdash;`) in any text meant to be read by an external
human — cover letters, application emails, anything similar. Use a plain
hyphen (` - `), comma, or colon instead, depending on what reads naturally.

**Why:** some readers treat em-dash usage as a tell that text was
AI-generated and react negatively to it, independent of the actual content
quality. Reducing that surface is a reasonable default, not overcaution.

**How to apply**: default to no em-dashes when drafting anything
externally-facing from the start, rather than writing them and fixing on
request. Internal writing (code comments, memory files, this session's own
prose) is unaffected — this is specifically about text a third party reads
without knowing an LLM was involved in drafting it.
