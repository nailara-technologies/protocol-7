owner 2026-11-16 : `bin/harmony <name>` \ `bin/is-true` may be used [ and offered to agents ] to check names, as an oracle or hint, not a hard rule -- a whole group of names can't always be made all-true logically.

applied : the resource ledger [ data/md/design/RESOURCE-ACCOUNTING-UNIFIED.md ] uses lowercase nested modules under `AMOS7::NRT` as a namespace root only [ AMOS7::NRT itself is FALSE, no NRT.pm ] : ::journal ::move\::transaction ::frame ::drops ::user::account[::transaction] ::market[::rate] all TRUE.

**Why:** owner values harmonic names but knows full consistency is not always possible.
**How to apply:** check candidate names, prefer TRUE, keep a FALSE when meaning or group consistency needs it and say so.
