reasoning.tree design seeds
five directions for visual generation tools
============================================================

seed 1: the iris / vortex overhead
A galactic iris viewed from directly above: 63 concentric rings radiating from a
white-gold center point, ring colors shifting from violet-blue at the inner rings
through cyan and green to amber at the outermost edge. Ring 7 is a slightly dimmer
gap — the void ring. Three to five luminous spiral arms curve counter-clockwise
from outer rings toward the center, each arm a thin bright arc in a different
namespace hue. The background is deep space near-black with subtle blue-violet
undertones. The viewer's eye should go to the center first, then follow an arm
CCW without being told to.

seed 2: the standing wave / omega gate
A resonant cavity shown in side view: horizontal axis from alpha (left, warm
amber) to omega (right, bright gold vertical gate line). A warm forward wave
crests above center axis as a smooth bezier curve; a cool violet return wave
gamma curves below. At the right edge, the forward wave curls into the return
in a tight luminous U-curve where the two touch at the gate. Near omega,
alternating bright and dark vertical interference bands represent the harmonic
impression omega holds. The superposition of both waves creates a luminous
neutral standing wave between them. The feeling: something alive, breathing.

seed 3: the full tree blueprint / ASCII holographic form
A dense monospace text field rendered as a living document — not a software
diagram but a star map made of characters. Expanded nodes glow violet-blue
with gold [:< markers; compact nodes recede to 50% opacity with dim >:|
markers. Tree connectors (│ ├─ └─) form the topology. The color temperature
tells a story the text doesn't repeat. Deep space background, tight leading,
every character load-bearing. The aesthetic: old terminal at 3am, musical
scores, circuit diagrams — something that lives between all of them.

seed 4: the convergence monitor / ambient peripheral display
A dark panel glowing quietly in a corner: a small concentric iris (12 rings)
at center left, a scrolling event log in monospace on the right with entries
like "47K3R4 [!] tree.insert THRESHOLD [:<" in dim text with gold flashes for
crossings. Below, a row of thin colored gauge bars showing convergence levels,
one with a gold threshold marker. The panel feels like a secondary monitor in
a dark room — ignorable when quiet, unmistakable when something completes.
The overall mood: the system narrating itself without demanding attention.

seed 5: the synthesis / all seven representations as facets
A single deep-space composition showing all seven views simultaneously as
facets of one system: the ASCII tree in the lower left, the iris overhead as
a circular frame enclosing the center, the standing wave as a horizontal
band across the middle, the cross-section as vertical depth strata, the chain
trace as a winding path through the scene, the explorer interface as layered
panels, and the monitor as a small inset. All unified by the same palette:
deep space black, violet-blue namespace hues, gold threshold markers, amber
forward signals, cool violet gamma returns. The feeling: looking at a living
system from every angle at once, and recognizing it is the same organism.
