Build a full-page standing wave visualization.

LAYOUT:
  horizontal canvas, full viewport
  alpha (left edge) to omega (right edge)
  center horizontal axis — the zero line, very dim

ELEMENTS:

  forward wave (alpha → omega):
    warm amber bezier curve, slightly above center axis
    flows left to right continuously (animation)
    color: #F39C12 with glow rgba(243,156,18,0.4)

  gamma return (omega → alpha):
    cool violet bezier curve, slightly below center axis
    flows right to left continuously (animation)
    color: #8A2BE2 with glow rgba(138,43,226,0.4)

  superposition (both waves combined):
    luminous neutral curve between the two
    color: #C8A96E — the warm-neutral blend
    slightly thicker line (4px vs 2px for individual waves)

  omega gate (right edge):
    bright vertical line, white-gold #FFE5A0
    the two waves meet here in a tight luminous U-curve
    the U-curve is the most visually striking element
    forward wave curves INTO the return wave at this point
    the U glows brighter than anything else in the scene

  interference bands (near omega, right 15% of canvas):
    alternating bright/dim vertical bands
    represents the harmonic memory omega holds
    resonant bands: gold at 60% opacity
    transform bands: near-black at 80% opacity
    subtle — visible but not competing with the U-curve

  alpha label (left edge): small "α", warm amber color
  omega label (right edge): small "Ω", white-gold color
  gamma label (along the return wave): small "γ", cool violet, italic

ANIMATION:
  both waves flow continuously
  forward: left-to-right motion
  gamma: right-to-left motion (opposite)
  the U-curve at omega: gentle pulse (±5% brightness, 2s cycle)
  interference bands: very subtle shimmer
  overall feeling: something alive, breathing

PROSE (bottom of canvas, small monospace):
  "omega is not the end. it was always the gate."
  color: var(--gold), 70% opacity, centered

PALETTE (same as iris.v7.ax/vortex/):
  --bg: #03030a
  --gold: #ffe5a0
  --violet: #9b7fd6
  --text-dim: #6f6688
  JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono / Courier New

TWEAKS PANEL (use same tweaks-panel.jsx from iris.v7.ax/vortex/):
  wave speed (0.5x to 3x)
  wave amplitude (how far above/below center axis)
  interference intensity (0 to 1)
  show/hide individual waves (forward, gamma, superposition toggles)
  show/hide labels

OUTPUT FILES:
  standing-wave.jsx
  index.html (loads tweaks-panel.jsx + standing-wave.jsx)

TITLE: "omega gate · iris.v7.ax"

SUCCESS TEST:
  someone sees the image and understands intuitively that the right edge
  is not an endpoint but a reflection — the forward wave does not stop,
  it turns and becomes the return. the U-curve makes this undeniable.
  the two colors (warm/cool) tell the story without words.
